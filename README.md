## 🎭 The Drama Universe

### 1. 🌸 [The Rise of the Benevolent Documentation Dictator](index.html) (Imperial C-Drama)
An imperial technical satire chronicling the trials and triumphs of the **Imperial Storage Bureau**.
- **博先生 (Bó Xiānsheng / Master Bob)** — Sage of the Scroll Repository who meditates beneath waterfalls whenever requirements drift.
- **云姑娘 (Yún Gūniang / Lady Yun)** — Keeper of the Cloud Scrolls, legendary for writing flawless documentation on the first draft.
- **顾公子 (Gù Gōngzǐ / Lord Gu)** — Minister of Requirements, impeccably groomed, suffering from terminal Second Lead Syndrome.
- **凌大人 (Líng Dàrén / Lord Ling)** — Keeper of Future Roadmaps and professional nemesis carrying kingdom-wide architecture alone.
- **无名长老会 (The Council of Forty-Seven Elders)** — The eternal bureaucratic review committee.

### 2. 🇰🇷 [Master Bob and the Monster in the Data Center](monster-in-data-center.html) (Makjang K-Drama)
A 16-episode makjang K-drama satire of knowledge, memory, and repository migrations in the **Cloud Realm**.
- **밥 도사 (Bob Dosa / Master Bob)** — Arrives dramatically from the mountain waterfall with Key (SHINee) energy: *"The issue is not the issue."*
- **윤 아키텍트 (Yun Akitekteu / Lady Yun)** — Architect of Knowledge Futures, carrying the cloud-sealed migration ledger and a centuries-old secret.
- **늪지의 마녀 (Neupji-ui Manyeo / The Bog Witch)** — Guardian of the Forgotten, protecting burning drive runbooks and 2016 floppy procedures.
- **문서냥이 (Munseo Nyangi / Documentation Cat)** — Finder of missing requirements and lost diagrams (cannot find the Single Source of Truth).
- **데이터센터의 괴물 (Deiteo Senteoui Goemul / The Monster)** — Sentient Living Archive born from every time someone said *"We'll document it later."*

### 3. 🦚 [Master Bob and the Festival of Validation](festival-of-validation.html) (Bobbywood Musical Spectacle)
A 2½-hour song-and-dance cinematic spectacle celebrating the eternal golden rule: ***Documentation is Trust***.
- **मास्टर बॉब (Master Bob)** — Wandering Repository Auditor & Keeper of the Golden Validation Seal (*"Documentation is trust written down."*).
- **लेडी युन (Lady Yun)** — High Priestess of Validation & Keeper of the Sacred Red Pen of Review (*"Show me the evidence."*).
- **लॉर्ड गु (Lord Gu Prakash Anand Malhotra)** — Herald of Readiness & Voice of the Festival (*"We are ready!"*).
- **लॉर्ड लिंग (Lord Ling Govind Tiwari Malhotra / L.G.T.M.)** — Minister of Accelerated Approvals & Patron Saint of Rubber-Stamp Reviews (*"Looks Good To Me."*).
- **राजकुमारी पूर्वधारणा (Princess Assumption)** — Believer in Tribal Knowledge (*"Nobody needs documentation."*).
- **अदृश्य समीक्षक (The Phantom Reviewer)** — The Invisible Approver (*"Calendar invite pending."*).
- **सत्यापन शून्यता (The Verification Void)** — The Great Antagonist born from skipped reviews and missing evidence.
