The Rise of the Benevolent Documentation Dictator

A Tale of Scrolls, Storage, Unsupported Configurations, and Moving Release Dates

Screenplay & Concept: Viktoria Muirhead Starring: 博先生 (Master Bob) & 云姑娘 (Lady Yun) Genre: Imperial C-Drama / Technical Satire / Romantic Bureaucracy Seasons: 4 Epic Seasons + Holiday Special

🌸 The Essential C-Drama Mandate

Because it is a C-drama, every simple relationship must inevitably evolve into a twelve-layer misunderstanding involving professional rivalry, ministry politics, and accidental eye contact. There must be romance, unnecessary bloodshed, and at least one pivotal scene where two characters stare at each other six feet apart for exactly 47 seconds while cherry blossom petals descend in slow motion.

The Cast

Meet the scholars, ministers, nemeses, and shadow councils that rule the Imperial Storage Bureau.

博先生 (Bó Xiānsheng)
Master Bob, Sage of the Scroll Repository
Protagonist · Dictator-in-Training
"No ministry functions this efficiently. What are your true intentions?"
  • Origins: Lowly archive keeper who uncovers ancient documentation methodologies.
  • Superpower: Relentless scroll rigor; immune to hand-waving and unverified assumptions.
  • Chosen Coping Mechanism: Whenever faced with undocumented requirements, unresolved dependencies, or shifting roadmaps: 🚶 walks into the mountains to meditate beneath a waterfall.
云姑娘 (Yún Gūniang)
Lady Yun, Keeper of the Cloud Scrolls
Brilliant Scholar · Rival Ministry
"Writing flawless documentation on the first draft is not witchcraft; it is vigilance."
  • Origins: High scholar from the rival Ministry of Infrastructure.
  • Secret Duty: Sole guardian of the kingdom's ancient Storage Archives.
  • Reputation: Writes flawless documentation on the first draft, making Master Bob deeply suspicious.
顾公子 (Gù Gōngzǐ)
Lord Gu, Minister of Requirements
The Impeccable Rival · 2nd Lead Syndrome
"...I documented them in the requirements."
  • Profile: Handsome, brilliant, impeccably dressed; can recite every policy scroll from memory.
  • The Red Flag: Delivers requirements before development starts (a practice completely alien to the realm).
  • The Plot Twist: Secretly leaves clues in old scrolls to guide Master Bob and protect Lady Yun.
凌大人 (Líng Dàrén)
Lord Ling, Keeper of Future Roadmaps
Professional Nemesis · Tragic Visionary
"Excellent. We've changed the architecture. Also the timeline. Also the feature scope."
  • Nature: Master Bob's eternal professional nemesis.
  • Weapon of Choice: Announcing strategic realignment immediately following final sign-off.
  • Tragic Backstory: Carries the burden of kingdom-wide storage crises alone beneath midnight waterfalls.
无名长老会

The Villain: The Council of Forty-Seven Elders

A mysterious, all-powerful body whose members materialize instantly whenever progress threatens to occur. They answer only to the Left and Right Chancellors, and there is always exactly one elder sitting firmly on the fence.

Formidable Martial Arts & Bureaucratic Abilities:

  • The Endless Echo: Asking questions already thoroughly answered in previous scrolls.
  • Temporal Unraveling: Reopening settled architectural decisions forty-eight hours before launch.
  • Summoning Jutsu: Convening daily high-focus emergency assemblies dedicated strictly to nothing.
  • Scroll Multiplication: Requesting additional peer reviews without having read the System Requirements Brief.

📜 The Immutable C-Drama Law of Lord Gu

Lord Gu stands before the court holding everything an engineer or technical writer could ever dream of:

  • ✅ Complete, unambiguous functional requirements
  • ✅ Full upstream and downstream dependency mapping
  • ✅ Signed stakeholder approvals from all four ministries
  • ✅ Quantitative risk assessment matrix
  • ✅ Realistic, achievable implementation plan

...and he is still losing the girl to a man who periodically disappears into the mountains..

Because C-drama universal law dictates: Most Qualified Candidate ≠ Successful Romantic Outcome. It is written in the stars. It cannot be changed. Not even Lord Ling can rewrite that roadmap.

📖 Fandom Wiki · Lore Archive
Fan Theory #3: Did Lord Gu Know?

Some viewers believe Lord Gu secretly knew the location of the Ancient Storage Archives years before Master Bob and Lady Yun discovered them.

Evidence includes:

  • His unusual knowledge of forgotten requirements.
  • The preservation of numerous lost scrolls.
  • His habit of appearing whenever critical documentation is needed.

Producers have neither confirmed nor denied the theory.

The Waterfall Chronicles (The Proven Methodology)

Everyone assumes Master Bob is the sensible, grounded protagonist. Then, the audience slowly discovers that Master Bob is, in fact, the most dramatic character in the seven realms.

Whenever faced with:

  • Undocumented requirements
  • Unresolved cross-team dependencies
  • Sudden architecture changes
  • Release date movement
  • Executive ambiguity

His chosen coping mechanism is apparently: 🚶 Walk into the mountains and stand under a waterfall.

Complete Episode Highlights

Episode 1 · The Scroll in the Vault Origins

A lowly archive keeper discovers ancient documentation techniques and rises through the ranks of the Imperial Storage Bureau.

Key moment: Bob rejects a draft written entirely in colloquial folklore.
Episode 2 · The Snapshot Heresy Political Intrigue

The Documentation Dictator faces court intrigue after discovering the kingdom's snapshots restore differently depending on hidden backend architecture.

Key moment: Bob demands backend parity before the imperial seal is applied.
Episode 3 · The Rogue Pull Request Rivalry

A rival faction creates a pull request before requirements have been finalized. Master Bob and Lady Yun clash across the conference courtyard.

Key moment: A 47-second silent stare as cherry blossom petals drift between them.
Episode 4 · The Unread Brief Bureaucracy

Forty-seven elders convene an emergency review board. Not a single elder has actually read the System Requirements Brief (SRB).

Key moment: 24 minutes spent debating font color while the deployment gate remains locked.
Episode 5 · The Decree of the Void Crisis

An imperial decree announces a feature release to the entire realm. The decree contains no implementation details whatsoever. Lady Yun covertly intervenes.

Key moment: Lady Yun replaces the flawed scroll moments before it is presented to the Emperor.
Episode 6 · The Four Ministries of Discord Impasse

Four ministries fiercely debate component ownership while the documentation scrolls remain completely unwritten.

Key moment: The discovery that each ministry believed the other was drafting the user guide.
Episode 7 · The Thread of Three Moons Investigation

The Sage Bob uncovers critical functional requirements buried within an unresolved comment thread dating back three moons.

Key moment: Master Bob and Lady Yun's lanterns illuminate the same ancient Slack thread.
Episode 8 · The Sea of VERIFY Suspense

A breathless messenger arrives proclaiming, "The draft is complete!" Upon unrolling the parchment, it consists entirely of [VERIFY] statements.

Key moment: Master Bob reaches for his meditation mat.
Episode 12 · The Waterfall of Unresolved Dependencies Masterpiece

Master Bob vanishes into the high peaks for three days following another roadmap decree. Lady Yun organizes a search party, only to find Lord Gu completely unfazed.

Key moment: Master Bob in flowing robes beneath the cascading falls, watching a scroll drift downstream.
Episode 18 · The Midnight Waterfall Revelation Plot Twist

The audience discovers why Lord Ling stands alone beneath a midnight waterfall. A 5-year-old scroll reveals he foresaw the Storage Crisis all along and shouldered the kingdom's burden alone.

Key moment: Six-minute flashback montage with cinematic guzheng soundtrack. Viewers declare him their favorite character.
Episode 24 · The Waterfall of Strategic Realignment Philosophy

Master Bob follows Lord Ling into the misty mountains. Beneath the Falls of Revised Priorities, Lord Ling reveals: "The roadmap was merely a roadmap."

Key moment: Viewers spend the next week writing philosophical essays trying to decipher what it means.
Episode 36 · Some Requirements Are Worth Keeping Fandom Meltdown

Lady Yun presents Master Bob with the ancient scroll preserved across ten thousand revisions. The single highest-rated episode in television history.

Key moment: 10/10 drama. Fandom explodes. Lord Gu fans enter mourning.
Special Holiday Episode · The Post-GA Decree Holiday Special

The Imperial Review Board convenes to request architectural redesigns after the release has already reached General Availability (GA).

Key moment: Master Bob is seen packing his bags for a remote mountain monastery.

Iconic Screenplay Excerpts

[Scene: The Grand Courtyard of Requirements. Lord Gu approaches, impeccably groomed, bearing a spotless silk scroll.]
MASTER BOB
No ministry functions this efficiently. What are your true intentions?
LORD GU
...I documented them in the requirements.
MASTER BOB
Exactly. Nobody does that.
[Lord Gu smiles enigmatically and adjusts his embroidered silk sleeve. The audience swoons.]
[Scene: Episode 12. Lady Yun rushes into the Imperial Storage Bureau. Master Bob has been missing for three days.]
JUNIOR OFFICIAL
Master Bob received another decree from Lord Ling.
LADY YUN
And?
JUNIOR OFFICIAL
The feature changed.
LADY YUN
Again?
JUNIOR OFFICIAL
Again.
LADY YUN
Where is Master Bob?
JUNIOR OFFICIAL
...the waterfall.
LADY YUN
...of course.
[Lady Yun turns to Lord Gu, who is calmly reviewing an immaculate risk matrix.]
LADY YUN
Should we go after him?
LORD GU
This is the third time this quarter.
LADY YUN
The third time?
LORD GU
The architecture review was the second.
[Cut to scenic wide shot: Master Bob standing beneath a raging waterfall in flowing azure robes. Cherry blossoms drift by. A single scroll floats peacefully downstream.]
MASTER BOB (VOICE-OVER)
"Requirements come and go. Architectures rise and fall. Release dates drift like leaves in the wind."
[Cut to Marquis Ling's chambers.]
MESSENGER
My Lord, Master Bob has vanished.
LORD LING
Check the waterfall.
MESSENGER
How did you know?
LORD LING
I changed the roadmap.
MESSENGER
Ah.
[Scene: Episode 24 — The Falls of Revised Priorities. Mist curls around the rocks. Master Bob finds Lord Ling staring into the abyss.]
LORD LING
If the people hate me, so be it. Better they curse my name than watch the kingdom's architecture fail.
MASTER BOB
Why did you never tell us?
LORD LING
You never asked.
[Waterfall intensifies. Dramatic guzheng solo swells.]
LORD LING
The roadmap was never the goal.
MASTER BOB
Then what was?
LORD LING
The roadmap was merely a roadmap.
MASTER BOB
... profound.
[Viewers spend the next seven days on discussion forums trying to explain what this meant. Nobody succeeds. 10/10 television.]
[Scene: Episode 36 — The Grand Pavilion of Version Control. Midnight lanterns sway gently in the breeze. Lady Yun withdraws an ancient, pristine scroll from her sleeve.]
MASTER BOB
You preserved this all these years? Through 47 review boards and 18 waterfall retreats?
LADY YUN
Some requirements are worth keeping.
[Their eyes meet across a distance of exactly six feet. Petals descend for 47 consecutive seconds. The audience immediately forgets how human language works. Social media crashes worldwide.]
[Scene: The Final Imperial Assembly. Forty-seven Elders gather around the Great Jade Table as the deployment bell tolls.]
COUNCIL ELDER #34
We have one final comment before the kingdom can deploy.
MASTER BOB
What is it?
COUNCIL ELDER #34
Have we considered the impact on documentation?
[A deafening silence falls across the entire imperial court. Every official, scholar, minister, and general turns in unison to stare directly at Master Bob. Petals freeze in mid-air. Fade to black.]
